## Lecture Materials

Lecture slides and supporting materials for Week 12.

## Lab Materials

Hands-on exercises involving attention mechanisms and sequence modeling.

## Topics Covered

- Limitations of RNNs and LSTMs
- Encoder-Decoder Architectures
- Attention Mechanisms
- Soft Attention
- Context Vectors
- Sequence-to-Sequence Models
- Neural Machine Translation
- Applications of Attention Networks

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:

- Explain the limitations of traditional RNN models
- Understand encoder-decoder architectures
- Describe how attention mechanisms work
- Apply attention-based models to sequence tasks
- Compare attention networks with traditional sequence models
- Understand the role of attention in modern deep learning systems
